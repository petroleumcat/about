About

Petroleum Cat Productions is an independent animation and art studio run by Varen (Mahyar).

This is the official website of Petroleum Cat Productions. It serves as the studio's central place for information about its projects, workflow, pipeline, production, and other activities.

As a small independent studio, Petroleum Cat Productions also takes an open and technical approach to its work. The website and its technical infrastructure are documented publicly where practical.
